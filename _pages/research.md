## Accessible Clothing Shopping with AI Smart Glasses

**CHI 2027 Full Paper — Under Review**  
*Accessibility · Wearable AI · Human–AI Interaction*

Investigating how AI-powered smart glasses can support blind and low-vision people's in-store clothing shopping experiences. The research includes interviews with ten blind and low-vision participants, an AI-assisted shopping prototype using Meta Ray-Ban smart glasses, and an interaction-focused user study.

[Research details](/projects/accessible-shopping/)

## AI Agents vs. LLMs for Translation Tasks

**CHI 2026 Poster — Accepted**  
*Human–AI Interaction · AI Agents · LLMs*

Exploring how task complexity and user self-efficacy influence experiences with AI agents and LLM-based assistance for translation tasks. The study included a within-subjects evaluation with sixteen participants.
