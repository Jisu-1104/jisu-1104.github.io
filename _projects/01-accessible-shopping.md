**Research · CHI 2027 Full Paper (Under Review)**

This project investigates how AI-powered smart glasses can support in-store clothing shopping for blind and low-vision people.

**Research activities:** Interviews with 10 blind and low-vision participants; research and prototyping with Meta Ray-Ban smart glasses; evaluation of AI-assisted shopping interactions and tasks.

**Topics:** Accessibility, Wearable AI, Human–AI Interaction.
