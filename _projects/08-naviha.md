**Undergraduate Project · Unity · Vuforia · Gyroscope**

Developed an AR-supported indoor navigation experience for Ewha Womans University's ECC building. Features include floor-overlaid footsteps, destination selection for classrooms, facilities and exits, and navigation-related information overlays.

**Contribution:** AR navigation implementation (shared responsibility for the navigation screen).
